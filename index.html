MRE HOA Tools

MRE HOA Tools is a private command-line tool used by the volunteer secretary of the Miner's Ravine Estates Homeowners Association (Loomis, California). It helps prepare meeting notices, agendas and minutes, and find HOA records in the association's own Google account.

It is not offered to the public. Only accounts controlled by the association's officers sign in to it.
